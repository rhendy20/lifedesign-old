# The PPE cart and the two rooms nobody would take

_2020, COVID_

March twenty-twenty I finally took charge. And the only reason I took it is because Debra left and they put this — they put a girl in who was, she was a good nurse, she was smart, but she was scared and it was coming off her, and when the charge nurse is scared the whole department is scared, it comes down the ceiling like gas. So the thing I remember most, and this is gonna sound petty — it's about a cart. We had two rooms at the end of the hall, seventeen and eighteen, negative pressure, and everything suspected went down there. Clustering care. She had never clustered anything in her life. She had a clipboard.

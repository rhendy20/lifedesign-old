# The night Nick was born

_November 1991_

Nick was born November, uh, twelfth. Ninety-one. Hamot.

I was on seconds then, three to eleven-thirty, and we had a job — forty-six parts, aluminum housings, and the whole lot had to be on a truck Wednesday morning or they were gonna charge us the freight for expedite. And I had eleven left when Deb's sister called the guard shack.

So I went. It's what, nine minutes from the plant to Hamot if you catch State right. And then you sit. She was in there a long time. They didn't let you in the same as they do now, or, I don't know, maybe they did and I was out in the hall. It was a long hallway with the chairs bolted together, like at the tire place.

He come at, uh. Quarter after four in the morning, roughly. Seven pounds one. And they had him in the plastic bin thing with the light and I stood there.

And then I went back in and ran the eleven. Got 'em on the truck. Because if you're gonna do it you do it, and the second shift guy, Rigo, he couldn't hold a tenth to save his life, he'd have scrapped 'em and then it's your name on the traveler anyway.
